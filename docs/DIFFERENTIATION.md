# Product Differentiation

## Category

**Predictive Discovery** is a proposed category between semantic data intelligence, AutoML, feature platforms, and decision intelligence.

## Adjacent capabilities

### AutoML
AutoML searches models, preprocessing, hyperparameters, and sometimes engineered features **given a dataset**.

Predictive Discovery asks whether the dataset itself is information-constrained and searches for additional governed signal.

### Semantic layers / ontologies
Semantic layers explain what enterprise data means and how entities relate.

Predictive Discovery consumes that semantic structure to search for information relevant to a prediction problem and then tests whether it actually helps.

### Catalogs
Catalogs help humans and agents find datasets.

Predictive Discovery turns discovery into a controlled predictive experiment.

### Feature stores
Feature stores govern, reuse, serve, and often point-in-time join known ML features.

Predictive Discovery can consume a feature store, but its goal is to identify **unknown or underused signal** and quantify its marginal value.

### Agentic analytics
Analytics agents answer questions and execute analytical workflows.

Predictive Discovery maintains a scientific loop whose objective is improved, defensible out-of-sample prediction.

## Defensible differentiation

The strongest differentiation is the integration of:

- residual-driven hypothesis generation;
- ontology/semantic-graph search;
- join-path reasoning;
- point-in-time feature availability;
- controlled feature-family experiments;
- stability and uncertainty analysis;
- marginal information value;
- economic Value of Information;
- autonomous next-experiment planning.

Any single component can be replicated. The system behavior emerges from the contracts and feedback loop between them.

## Product positioning

Short:

> **Predictive Discovery finds the information your model is missing—and proves whether it is worth using.**

Technical:

> A platform-neutral autonomous predictive-science framework that searches governed semantic data, validates candidate information, experimentally measures incremental out-of-sample value, and converts predictive uplift into economic Value of Information.

Executive:

> Tell it what the business needs to predict. It determines what information matters, where that information exists, whether it improves the prediction, and whether it is worth the investment.

## What we should not claim

Do not claim:
- automatic causal discovery;
- guaranteed best possible model;
- that semantic similarity proves predictive value;
- that high feature importance proves business value;
- that a candidate dataset should be purchased before controlled testing;
- that the system can know information availability without explicit metadata or a defensible inference.

The product is strongest when it is rigorous about uncertainty.
